# Pillender
Imagine you’re a healthy college student who suddenly catches pneumonia. On top of midterm stress, you now have to juggle a strict, 7-day antibiotic routine. Remembering this new habit is tough, but it's critical. That's why we're building Pillendar.

Our app uses image recognition to seamlessly scan your prescription and automatically build a personalized calendar that reminds you to take your medication on time. Sticking to your schedule ensures a smooth recovery, while missed doses can be harmful. Studies show that 7–20% of patients miss their scheduled medications, which can lead to severe issues like antibiotic resistance. Pillendar is here to change that.
